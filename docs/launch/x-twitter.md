## 2/

> It teaches by ear, not from a chart.
>
> Memorising A = .- teaches you to translate: hear beeps, convert to dots and
> dashes, look up the letter. That extra step is what makes people stall. So
> every character is seen, heard, then sent back with your own keyboard.

**Attach:** `assets/07-lesson-stage-1-1200x630.png`
**Alt:** "Stage 1 of 7 in the lesson: seven stages from E T to 0–9, See, Hear
and Send steps, and the letter E on CRT glass."
