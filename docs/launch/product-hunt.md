## Maker's first comment

> Hi Product Hunt 👋 I'm Can.
>
> I'm learning to sail in Turkey, my course requires an amateur radio
> certificate, and
> for me that came with a requirement: Morse code. A chart teaches you to
> *translate*: hear beeps, convert to dots and dashes, look up the letter. What
> worked was learning each character by its sound, so I turned that method into
> an app.
>
> MorseForge teaches by sound from the first minute, and the front page is
> the first lesson: tap the key once and you've sent E. Each character after
> that is seen, heard, and sent back with your own keyboard, seven stages from
> E and T to all 26 letters and 10 digits.
>
> A few things I'm happy with:
> 📨 The 60-second test makes a link with the same characters in the same order,
> so your friend's score actually compares with yours.
> 📅 The daily is the same 8 lines for everyone, and the share gives nothing away.
> 💡 Lamp mode: every listening drill works with the sound off.
> 🔒 No account, no ads, works offline. Progress stays in your browser.
>
> It's free and open source (MIT). It keys as a straight key or as iambic
> paddles, and it's English only for now.
>
> What would make you come back on day two? That's the question I most want
> answered.
