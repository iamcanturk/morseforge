**Length:** about 70 s (story ~25 s, feature tour ~40 s, end ~5 s). **Voice:** Can's ElevenLabs clone (`eleven_v4`),

## Story points Can must confirm

These are Can's own story. If any isn't exactly how it happened, change or cut
it.

1. He's taking an amateur sailing course, and the course requires an amateur
   radio certificate.
2. Getting that certificate required Morse for him.
3. He started by memorising the chart, and it didn't stick: hear the beep,
   picture the dots, look up the letter.
4. What worked was learning each letter by its sound: shortest codes first,
   full speed, room between letters.
5. He's still learning.

> Confirmed by Can (2026-10-21): the requirement is for his course in Turkey.
> The English copy says "here in Turkey", so it reads as his situation and not
> as a rule for everyone.
